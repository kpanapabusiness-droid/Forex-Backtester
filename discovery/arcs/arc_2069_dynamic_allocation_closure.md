**It is closed — by composition, two independent arguments, either sufficient:**

1. **The two opposite-demand folds are ex-ante observationally CO-REGIME.** 2015 and 2018 are *both*
   risk-off / strong-USD / vol-expansion years (2067). The only feature that distinguishes them is
   whipsaw-vol (2015, SNB de-peg) vs directional-trend-vol (2018, USD grind) — established in 1062. So any
   ex-ante regime variable an allocator could read (realized vol LEVEL, dispersion, USD-basket direction)
   does NOT separate them; the lone separator (trend-vs-whipsaw, e.g. an efficiency ratio) is exactly the
   axis LESSONS records as failed across three measures with "strong-trend regimes INVERT." No causal regime
   signal cleanly routes weight to fbr in 2015 and to me_short in 2018.

2. **Even granting a perfect separator, the favor-this-leg-in-this-fold MAPPING is fold-sign lookahead.**
   You only know "2015 wants fbr, 2018 wants me_short" from having seen the per-fold *outcomes*. With exactly
   **n = 2 binding folds**, any regime rule that "works" is fit to two data points = pure arc-1021
   weight-paint / overfit, indistinguishable from luck out of sample. This is the 1036 fold-sign-blindness
   result generalized from a scalar to a conditioned allocator: conditioning the allocation on which leg
   pays which fold is conditioning on the label.

I deliberately did **not** run a data fit of "best ex-ante regime variable separating the fold set" — with
n = 2 binding folds such a fit is overfit-guaranteed and would *manufacture* a spurious separation, the
exact arc-3004 / arc-1021 failure mode. The honest instrument here is the composition argument, not a
